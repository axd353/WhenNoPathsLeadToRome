We have put together a video series to help anyone who wants to download our hashtag#NeurIPS2025 paper dataset <https://lnkd.in/gsvHdMkY>, understand what it looks like, and start training reasoning models with it. The series also includes a few short lectures that walk through the paper we’re presenting at the conference and give a gentle introduction to how reasoning tasks are framed and what modern reasoning models are expected to do.
No prior knowledge is needed—jump right in if you’ve ever wanted to understand what “reasoning” means in the context of AI.
